Where a household
mints its plans

Vittam is not a record of one plan. It is a treasury: every strategy is a ledger you can strike, compare, and keep. The visual language borrows from the mint itself. Engraved linework, reeded edges, ledger rules, and figures set like denominations.

Neither theme is the other inverted. Paper carries deep viridian ink and muted brass. The vault carries spearmint and pale gold on green-black metal.

Plate III · Explore

Reading about an instrument should end in one tap

    Class edge. The asset-class color stays a thin engraved edge, as in the current app, but on the new palette.

  • b

    One-tap Add. 44px target. Tapping opens the holding form prefilled with the instrument. Card flips to Added.

  • c

    Rates carry dates. Any quoted rate shows when it was last verified, honoring the re-verify rule.

Before Phase 0

What this folio decides, and what it leaves open

  • Decided here, pending your veto: the mint and treasury language, paper and vault as the two themes, strategies as first-class ledgers on the dashboard, and the emergency fund as a hatched Reserve slice.
  • Open: whether Current stays a protected baseline that alternate ledgers cannot edit, or any ledger can be promoted to Current.
  • Open: the AI counsel budget. Everything marked Concept preview here renders with no model behind it, so the visual ships regardless.
  • Open: whether the bulk-import template carries member names, which means personal data inside a downloaded file.
  • Not in this folio: onboarding, Profile, and instrument detail. They inherit the same tokens once the language is approved.
